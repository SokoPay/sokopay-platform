## 1. Appointment

SokoPay appoints you as an agent to provide cash-in and cash-out services to SokoPay customers at the outlet registered with us. You act for SokoPay, which stays responsible to customers for agent services as the law requires.

## 2. Float

- You buy e-money float by paying SokoPay through the channels we name.
- A float top-up is credited after a **second SokoPay staff member confirms your payment arrived**.
- Your float is yours to use for customer transactions. It doesn't earn interest.

## 3. Serving customers

- **Cash in:** confirm the customer's name in the app before taking cash and crediting their wallet.
- **Cash out:**
  - The customer must first press Cash out in their app or on USSD.
  - You send the request.
  - The customer approves it with their own PIN.
  - **Only hand over cash after the app shows "Approved".**
- **Never ask for, look at or type a customer's PIN.**
- Display SokoPay's fees and complaint contacts at your outlet. Charge nothing beyond the published fees.
- Treat customers fairly and keep their information confidential.

## 4. Commission

- Commission is earned on eligible cash-in and cash-out transactions at the published rate [VERIFY schedule].
- It is paid into your float monthly.
- **No commission** is paid on transactions involving your own wallet, or on very small transactions designed to generate commission.
- Commission is held while your agency is suspended.

## 5. Compliance

- You must follow anti-money-laundering rules:
  - report suspicious activity to SokoPay;
  - don't split transactions to avoid limits;
  - don't serve someone you believe is acting for another person to hide funds.
- SokoPay monitors agent activity and may suspend you during an investigation.

## 6. Suspension and termination

SokoPay may suspend or end your appointment for fraud, customer harm, regulatory instruction or breach of these terms. On termination, your remaining float, less amounts owed, is paid to your nominated account after reconciliation.

## 7. Law

These terms are governed by the laws of Ghana.
