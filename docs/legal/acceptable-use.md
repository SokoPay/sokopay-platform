# Acceptable Use Policy

**Version 1.0 (draft) · Effective: [date of launch]**

> DRAFT FOR LEGAL REVIEW.

SokoPay must not be used:

- for anything illegal in Ghana, including fraud, scams, money laundering, terrorist financing, sanctions evasion, illegal gambling or illegal goods;
- to receive money for someone else to hide where it came from (acting as a "money mule");
- to split payments to stay under limits or reporting thresholds;
- with someone else's identity, phone number or Ghana Card, or by opening accounts for others;
- to harass anyone, including by sending payment prompts or requests to people who didn't ask for them;
- to attack, probe or overload SokoPay's systems, or to get around its security or limits;
- by merchants, to charge for goods or services not supplied, or to process other businesses' payments through their account.

SokoPay monitors transactions to detect these activities, as the law requires. We may hold funds, suspend accounts, refuse service and report to the Financial Intelligence Centre and law enforcement. The law may not allow us to tell you that we have made a report.

To report misuse, contact support@sokopay.com.gh [VERIFY].
