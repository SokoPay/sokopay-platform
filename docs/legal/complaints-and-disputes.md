We record every complaint and its outcome. We report complaint statistics to the Bank of Ghana.

## 3. Disputes about a business payment

1. Report the problem from the receipt within **120 days** of the payment.
2. The business has **7 days** to refund you or explain.
3. If they don't settle it, SokoPay reviews both sides and decides. The reason is recorded and shown to both sides.
4. If SokoPay decides for you, the refund comes from the business's SokoPay balance, back to the way you paid.

While the dispute is open, the disputed amount is held from the business's payouts.

## 4. Payments that went wrong on our side

- **A payment failed but money left your wallet:** it is refunded automatically. If you don't see it within 24 hours, complain with the reference.
- **Money sent to the wrong person through SokoPay:** we will try to recover it, but can't guarantee it. Always check the recipient's name before confirming.

## 5. Unauthorised transactions

If you didn't make a payment:
1. Sign out of all devices (More, then Security).
2. Change your PIN.
3. Report it straight away.

We may hold the wallet while we investigate.
