Fees & charges

Everything SokoPay charges, what it pays agents, and any discounts. A price can be free, a flat amount, a percentage capped at a figure, or flat + percentage. Every change and discount is proposed by one finance user and approved by another; nothing is back-dated, and customers see the fee before they confirm.
