How customer data is protected

• The AI can't read the database. It only gets totals from fixed reports (counts, values, rates).

• No names, phone numbers, ID numbers, wallet IDs, references or addresses are ever sent. Groups under 5 are shown as "<5".

• Questions and answers are scanned and anything that looks personal is removed, both ways.

• Limited to compliance, finance and operations; rate-limited; daily budget; every use is logged.

• Off unless an administrator switches it on.
