## 2. What we collect and why

We collect only what we need to run a payment service safely and to meet the law.

| Data | When we collect it | Why we need it | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mobile number | When you sign up | To identify your account, send one-time codes and alerts | Contract |
| Name | When you sign up, or from your Ghana Card | To show who you are paying and who paid you, and for identity checks | Contract and legal obligation |
| Ghana Card number | When you raise your limits | Identity verification ("Know Your Customer") required by the Bank of Ghana and anti-money-laundering law. It is **encrypted** and we keep a one-way fingerprint to stop one card opening many accounts | Legal obligation |
| Selfie photo | Only if you choose the highest account tier | Sent once to our identity partner to confirm you match your Ghana Card. **We do not keep the photo**, only the result (a score and a reference) | Legal obligation and consent |
| PIN | When you set it | To approve payments. It is stored only as a one-way hash we cannot read | Contract and security |
| Transactions | Whenever you pay, send, receive, top up, cash in or cash out | To move your money, keep accurate books, show your history and statements, handle disputes and refunds | Contract and legal obligation |
| Recipients you save | When you tap "Save recipient" | To make repeat payments quicker | Consent (you can delete them) |
| Device information | When you allow notifications | The notification token and phone type, to send alerts to your phone | Legitimate interest |
| Security records | Sign-ins, failed PIN attempts, 2FA events (portal users) | To protect your account and investigate fraud | Legitimate interest and legal obligation |
| Messages you send us | Disputes, complaints, support | To resolve your issue | Contract |
| Merchant information | When a business signs up | Business registration, tax number, owner identity, settlement account, to approve and pay the business | Contract and legal obligation |
| Agent information | When an agent is appointed | Identity, location of the outlet, float records, to run the agent network | Contract and legal obligation |

**What we do not collect.** We do not collect your location from your phone. We do not read your contacts. We do not collect your fingerprint or face: fingerprint and face unlock happen on your phone and never reach us. We do not sell your data or use it for third-party advertising.

**Camera.** The app uses the camera only when you scan a QR code or take a verification selfie.

## 4. Where your data is stored

Our servers are hosted by Amazon Web Services in the **Africa (Cape Town) region, South Africa**. This means your data is transferred outside Ghana. We do this under the safeguards required by Act 843 [VERIFY: confirm with the Data Protection Commission and the Bank of Ghana that offshore hosting is approved, or move to in-country hosting]. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest.

## 5. How long we keep it

- **Transaction and identity records:** kept for **at least 5 years after your account is closed**, as anti-money-laundering law requires [VERIFY period under Act 1044 and BoG directives].
- **After that period:** closed accounts are anonymised. The name is removed and the number replaced, while the anonymous accounting records remain.
- **Selfie photos:** never stored by us.
- **One-time codes:** expire within minutes.
- **Signed-out session tokens:** deleted after they expire.

## 6. How we protect it

- Encryption in transit and at rest; Ghana Card numbers encrypted with a separate key.
- PINs and passwords stored as one-way hashes (Argon2).
- Account lockout after repeated wrong PINs, and protection against SIM-swap takeover.
- Two-factor authentication for business owners, finance users and all SokoPay staff.
- Staff see only what their role needs. Sensitive actions need a second person to approve.
- Every movement of money is recorded in an audit-ready ledger.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach is likely to put you at risk, we will tell you and the Data Protection Commission without undue delay, as the law requires.

## 7. Your rights

Under Act 843 you can:

- **Access:** ask what personal data we hold about you. Statements are available in the app at any time.
- **Correction:** ask us to correct wrong data. You can edit your name in the app until your Ghana Card is verified.
- **Objection:** object to processing based on our legitimate interests.
- **Deletion:** ask us to delete your data. We delete or anonymise it unless the law requires us to keep it, as it does for financial records.
- **Close your account:** in the app under More, Security, Close account.
- **Withdraw consent:** for example, by deleting saved recipients or turning off notifications.
- **Complain:** to us first, then to the Data Protection Commission of Ghana.

To make a request, contact the Data Protection Officer. We may ask you to prove your identity, and we will respond within [VERIFY] days.
