## 1. Scope

These terms apply to businesses that accept payments through SokoPay:
- QR codes;
- payment links;
- website checkout;
- the merchant API;
- bulk payouts.

The SokoPay Terms of Service, Privacy Policy and Acceptable Use Policy also apply to your team members' accounts.

## 2. Onboarding

- You must give accurate business and owner information and documents.
- SokoPay may refuse or suspend any business, and runs identity, sanctions and risk checks before and after approval.
- You're responsible for who you add to your team and the role you give them:
  - **Owner:** full control.
  - **Admin:** manages the business.
  - **Finance:** money and settlements.
  - **Cashier:** takes payments.
  - **Developer:** test API keys.

## 3. Taking payments

- Only take payments for genuine sales of lawful goods and services you supply.
- Show customers your trading name so they recognise the payment.
- **Confirm payments by the SokoPay webhook or API**, not by a browser redirect or a screenshot.
- Keep API keys and webhook signing secrets confidential. Rotate them if they leak.

## 4. Fees and settlement

- **Fees:** the merchant discount rate (MDR) is deducted from each payment as shown in your portal [VERIFY tariff].
- **Settlement:** to your verified settlement account on the schedule shown [VERIFY: T+1]. Large or newly changed accounts need a SokoPay approval.
- **Disputed amounts:** held from settlement while a dispute is open.
- **Reserves:** SokoPay may hold a reserve or delay settlement for risk reasons, with notice where the law allows.

## 5. Refunds and disputes

- You may refund a payment, fully or in part, within 180 days. The money goes back to the customer the way they paid, from your SokoPay balance. **The fee isn't returned.**
- When a customer disputes a payment, you have 7 days to refund or explain. If you don't respond or settle it, SokoPay decides. If SokoPay decides for the customer, the amount is refunded from your balance.

## 6. Data

- You receive only the customer data needed for the sale. Phone numbers are masked.
- You must protect it, use it only for the sale, and comply with the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

## 7. Prohibited activity

See the Acceptable Use Policy. Breaches may lead to suspension, withheld funds and reports to the authorities.

- **Liability:** you are responsible for your goods and services, your staff, and the security of your systems and credentials.
- **Suspension:** SokoPay may suspend the account for suspected fraud, regulatory instruction or breach.
